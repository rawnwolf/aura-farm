Staking, without the lock.

Nothing to stake into, nothing to claim. You hold, and the farm pays you.

  1. Hold to farm

    Every hour you hold $AURA, you earn aura: your coins times your multiplier.

  2. Time beats size

    Your multiplier starts at 1×, reaches 2× at day 7 and 3× at day 30. Long holders out-farm whales who just arrived.

  3. Harvest every day

    Once a day, about a quarter of the bankroll is paid to holders by their share of total aura. It lands in your wallet. No claiming.

  4. Selling shatters your aura

    Any sell or transfer out resets your aura and multiplier to zero. Buying more never resets you.
